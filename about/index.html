About My Plastic Promise

In just under 10 minutes of litter picking at the beach, I found...

  • One popped balloon
  • Two bottle lids
  • One ball
  • One crabbing line
  • Three plastic cups
  • One tupperware box
  • One bottle of hair dye
  • One cleaning brush
  • Three shoes
  • And...
  • Eleven plastic bottles!

How crazy is that?!

As you may have guessed, all of these items are now in the bin.

This means our beach will look cleaner, be safer, and will be a nice environment for people to enjoy.

It also means our beach is safer for marine wildlife, birds, and any other animals that live and feed on the beach.
